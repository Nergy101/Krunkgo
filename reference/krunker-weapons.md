# Krunker.io Weapon & Combat Reference

Sourced from krunkerio.fandom.com (correct current domain — `krunker.fandom.com` 404s; `krunkerio.fandom.com` is live) and the NamuWiki datamined weapon-config tables (en.namu.wiki/w/Krunker/무기 — a community datamine of the weapon config JSON, NOT officially published, likely reflects an older patch snapshot). Where NamuWiki conflicts with the English wiki's own Patch History (which documents the latest balance numbers), the Patch History value is treated as current and the datamine value is kept as a fallback, labeled `[datamine]`.

**Units**: Krunker's engine units are not metres. No official hitbox-size or player-height document exists. Convention used here per task instructions: player ≈ 2 engine units tall, 1 block = 1 engine unit ⇒ 1 engine unit ≈ 0.9–1.0 m (assuming a ~1.8–2.0 m player). Range/spread numbers below are raw engine units unless noted `[approx m]`.

## Hitbox layout (head/body/limbs)

Per the [Damage](https://krunkerio.fandom.com/wiki/Damage) page's Body Part Multiplier table: **Torso 1.0x** (baseline reference — this is body/base damage), **Head: multiplier "dependent on weapon"** (varies per gun, see table above), **Arms 0.5x**, **Legs 0.5x**. Notable exceptions: Crossbow, Blaster, and Rocket Launcher **cannot activate body-part multipliers at all** regardless of hit location. Cosmetic items (wings etc.) do NOT alter the hitbox. Relative hitbox *sizes* (head box vs torso box vs limb box, in engine units) are **UNKNOWN** — no wiki page publishes exact hitbox dimensions; only the damage-multiplier-per-region is documented, not geometry.

## Hitmarker & kill feedback

- **Hit**: distinct hitmarker sound effect (asset filename `hit_0.mp3`), plus an optional on-screen floating damage-number popup showing exact damage dealt (customizable color, size slider 0.1–2x, default 1x — visual only, does not change actual damage).
- **Headshot**: separate, distinct headshot sound effect (asset filename `headshot_0.mp3`) plus a separately-colorable "critical hit" damage popup color option.
- **Kill**: a "Player Killed Player" message appears in the kill feed (enabled by default, toggle-able in Settings; disabling it surfaces more chat messages instead).

## Combat feel summary

- **Everything kills fast and cleanly**: nearly every primary drops a 100 HP target in 2–6 shots inside half a second — Krunker reads as an arcade headshot-hunter, not a bullet-sponge shooter; the Semi Auto (2-shot headshot kill in 150ms) and Revolver (2-shot kill in 450ms) exemplify the "burst precision" identity the class roster is built around.
- **Headshot multipliers are modest and weapon-specific, not universal**: several signature weapons (SMG, Akimbo Uzi, Blaster, Rocket Launcher) intentionally have **zero** headshot bonus, so "spray and pray" archetypes stay viable without needing pixel-perfect aim — this is a deliberate design axis, not an oversight.
- **Projectile weapons are rare and clearly telegraphed**: only Rocket Launcher, Crossbow, and Blaster are true projectiles (with slow, gravity-affected travel); everything else is instant hitscan, so "lead your shots" is a small, opt-in skill niche rather than the baseline expectation.
- **Class HP variance (60–170/180) is the real damage-sponge dial**: instead of armor or shields, Krunker tunes "tankiness" purely through per-class max HP, letting the same weapon numbers produce wildly different TTKs against different classes (e.g., Sniper one-shots everything ≤100 HP but needs two hits on Spray N Pray).
- **Feedback is loud and layered but the numbers behind it are thinly documented**: distinct hit/headshot sounds plus optional floating damage numbers and a kill feed give constant sensory confirmation, but core "feel" levers like respawn delay, spawn protection, and passive HP-regen rate are simply not published anywhere in the community wiki — worth measuring directly from actual play rather than trusting a source for those three specific numbers.
