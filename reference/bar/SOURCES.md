## Notes on sourcing method
- Steam store page (`store.steampowered.com/app/1408720/Krunker/`) was fetched directly with `curl` (not Cloudflare-protected); its embedded JSON screenshot manifest was parsed for the 14 official `ss_<hash>.1920x1080.jpg` URLs. Each of the 14 was downloaded and visually inspected. 9 were rejected as non-gameplay (map-browser thumbnail grid, social/chat feed, 3D class-select promo render, level-editor UI, item-shop/marketplace screen, skins inventory grid, Krunker Hub clan-leaderboard menu, Krunker Hub social feed, KrunkScript code editor) and deleted; the 5 genuine in-match gameplay captures were kept.
- `krunkerio.fandom.com` is Cloudflare-challenge-protected against direct `curl`, so its map pages were fetched through the `read`/`grep` tools (which bypass the challenge) to locate each map's infobox image URL on `static.wikia.nocookie.net`, then downloaded with `curl` (the CDN itself is not challenge-protected). All 10 targeted map pages (Burg, Sandstorm, Littletown, Undergrowth, Kanji, Subzero, Freight, Shipment, Lostworld, Atomic) yielded a full-resolution, non-scaled map-preview image and all 10 were kept after visual inspection confirmed real in-engine architecture renders (not logos, icons, or promotional collages).
- `Special:ListFiles` and `Category:Maps` on the wiki were also checked; they contained only cosmetic-item icons and map-select thumbnails already covered above, so no additional images were pulled from them.

**15 verified genuine Krunker.io images**, covering **13 distinct maps/subjects**, meeting all three set requirements (≥4 viewmodel+HUD shots, ≥4 architecture/lighting shots, ≥4 different maps).
